Super Store Sales Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Super Store sales and profitability performance.
The dashboard provides insights into sales, profit, quantity, customer segments, payment modes, shipping modes, categories, sub-categories, and regional performance.

Business Objective
The objective of this analysis is to:

- Understand overall sales and profit performance
- Identify high-performing customer segments
- Analyze sales trends over time
- Compare shipping and payment modes
- Identify top-performing product categories and sub-categories
- Analyze regional and state-level performance

Tools Used
- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Business Analysis

Key KPIs

| KPI | Value |
| Total Sales | 1.57M |
| Total Profit | 0.18M |
| Total Quantity | 22K |

📈 Dashboard Analysis

Customer Segment
The dashboard compares sales contribution across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

Sales Trend
Monthly sales performance is analyzed across 2019 and 2020 to identify changes in sales over time.

Profit Trend
Monthly profit performance is compared across years to understand profitability trends.

Shipping Mode
Sales are analyzed across Standard Class, Second Class, First Class, and Same Day shipping modes.

Product Performance
Category and sub-category analysis helps identify products contributing significantly to overall sales.

Regional Performance
The interactive region filter allows users to compare performance across Central, East, South, and West regions.

💡 Key Insights
- Consumer is the largest customer segment by sales contribution.
- Standard Class is the leading shipping mode by sales.
- Office Supplies is the highest-performing category by sales.
- Phones, Chairs, and Binders are among the leading sub-categories by sales.
- Monthly sales and profit show different patterns, highlighting the importance of analyzing profitability along with revenue.
